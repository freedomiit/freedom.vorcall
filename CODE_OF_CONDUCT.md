# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Disagree about the code, not
about the person writing it.

## What is expected

- Keep criticism about the work: the design, the code, the trade-off.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a feature and mean it. Vorcall has a small scope
  on purpose, and "out of scope" is not a personal judgement.
- Respect that people here have different levels of experience, different first languages
  and different amounts of time to give.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks — in issues, pull requests, commits or reviews.
- Discriminatory remarks about anyone's identity or background.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled decisions, spamming, or arguing in bad faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or delete anything that breaks the above, and may block
accounts that keep doing it. Serious or repeated behaviour gets a permanent ban without
further warning.

To report a problem, use GitHub's
[private reporting](https://github.com/freedomiit/freedom.vorcall/security/advisories/new)
or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are handled privately, and whoever reports one is
not required to interact with the person they are reporting.

This applies in every project space — the repository, its issues and pull requests,
releases and discussions — and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.
